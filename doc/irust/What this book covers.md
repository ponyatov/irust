# 1.1 What this book covers

In this book, I’ll present various idioms, patterns, and design patterns. Some of these patterns are specific to Rust; others are old ideas presented in a new format within the framework of Rust’s unique features, grammar, and syntax.

OOP often leads to excessive boilerplate code and overly complex patterns. Sometimes, we justify complexity for the sake of complexity in OOP, engaging in mental gymnastics. Complex systems, however, tend to fail faster and more dramatically than simple systems and are also more challenging to understand.

Rust’s language designers threw away much legacy OOP cruft, focusing instead on what’s needed to build quality software.
