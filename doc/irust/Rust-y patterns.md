Design patterns are powerful abstractions that every programmer can use to produce high-quality code. Humans are excellent at pattern recognition, and following well-understood and easily recognized patterns helps us solve two tricky problems: 
- reasoning about whether a design is good or bad
- helping other people understand our code.
