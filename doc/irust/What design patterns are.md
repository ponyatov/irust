# 1.2 What design patterns are

-  Design patterns are reusable.
-  Design patterns can be applied widely and broadly.
-  Design patterns solve problems in a way that makes it easy to reason about how someone else’s code works.
-  Design patterns are well understood by other experienced developers.
-  Code that doesn’t follow well-established patterns may fall under the category of antipatterns.

## [[What are antipatterns]]?

distinguish patterns from idioms
- idioms generally relate to the code itself
- patterns generally relate to the design and architecture of your software
